## Replacing them with real Shai Construction photos

Do this as soon as you have your own job-site photography — your real work will sell the
business better than any stock image, and it removes the attribution obligation entirely.

1. Drop the new files into `img/`. Match the ratios: hero **3:2** (1600×1067 and 900×600),
   services **4:3** (1000×750 and 600×450).
2. Update the two `<img>` tags in `index.html` (each is marked with a `<!-- PHOTO -->`
   comment) — change `src`, `srcset`, and the `alt` text to describe the actual photo.
3. Delete the "Photography:" line from the footer of all five pages.
4. Delete this file.

**Do not** add photographs of work that is not Shai Construction's own and present it as
such — implying someone else's project is yours is a misrepresentation, whatever the
image licence says.
