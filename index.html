Who we are

One contractor, start to finish

Shai Construction is a general contractor based in Richmond Hill, providing construction services since 2017. We work on residential and commercial projects of any size — from a single-room repair to a full commercial build-out — with the same three commitments on every job: quality workmanship, on-time completion, and customer satisfaction.

No matter the size of the project, our experienced team is ready to provide reliable and professional construction solutions.

How We Work

What we do

Nine services, one point of contact

Every trade below is scoped, sequenced, and managed by the same team, so the schedule holds together instead of falling between subcontractors.

General Construction Foundation-to-finish contracting for residential and commercial projects, from additions to ground-up structural work. Complete Home Renovations Full-scope renovations covering layout changes, structural work, and every finish trade in between. Basement Renovations Turning unfinished basements into livable, code-compliant space, including egress and waterproofing where needed. Kitchen & Cabinet Installation New layouts, custom cabinetry, and countertop installation for kitchens built to be used hard. Plumbing Rough-in, fixture installation, and repair work for kitchens, bathrooms, and mechanical rooms. HVAC Installation & Repair Furnace and air conditioning installation, ductwork, and repair to keep the whole house at temperature. Swimming Pool Installation In-ground pool construction from excavation through decking, fencing, permitting, and final fill. Landscaping Grading, hardscaping, and planting that finish the exterior to match the work done inside. Commercial Contracting Tenant fit-outs, renovations, and new build-outs for commercial and retail spaces.

Residential and commercial work across Richmond Hill, Vaughan, Markham, North York, Aurora, and Newmarket.

Why work with us

The standard every job is held to

Quality Workmanship

Every job is built to the standard it should be, not the standard that's fastest to finish. Materials, framing, and finish work are done right the first time.

On-Time Completion

Projects are scheduled with a real timeline set at the quote stage, and trades are sequenced to hold to it, so the date you're given is the date you can plan around.

Customer Satisfaction

The job isn't finished until you've walked the completed space and it matches the scope agreed to at the start.

Any Project Size

From a single repair to a full home renovation or commercial build-out, the same project management and standards apply regardless of scale.
